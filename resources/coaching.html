Coaching

Motorcycle Racing Association (Ireland) Ltd. encourages coaching of competitors in a safe and controlled enviornemtn so has developed a coaching strategy to support this. The policy is currently being revised and will be published online in the near future. The MRA plans to host/support a number of coaching events in the season so keep an eye on the home/events pages along with our social media for deatils closer to the time. Anyone can apply to become a MRA Accreditied Coach. If you are intered in applying or want to know more about the process, please get in touch by emailing, secretary@mraireland.com
