Race Licences

Annual Competiton licences fees are set annually at the MRA AGM and application forms are available from all affiliated clubs. You must be a member of an affiliated club to apply for an annual licence.

2026 Annual Competiton Licence

  • Adult Licence - £50
  • Youth Licence - £30
  • Youth (Family-2) - £40
  • Youth (Family-3) - £50

    • If an annual licence isn't for you, one event licences are available at all MRA events.

    2026 One Event Competition Licence

    Not redeemable against annual licence

  • Adult - £10
  • Youth - £10

    • *To be elgible for a one event licence, the competitor must reside at an address within Northern Ireland or the Republic of Ireland.
