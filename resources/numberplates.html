Number Plate Specificaition

Competitors are remidned that when competing at MRA Events, machine number plates must adhere to the backgound and number colour specifications outlined in the rule book. The specifications are as follows:

Adult Solo Classes

  • MX1 Class - White Backgounrd and Black Numbers
  • MX2 Class - Black Background and White Numbers
  • MX3 Class - Yelllow Background and Black Numbers

    • Adult Quad Classes

  • ALL - White Background and Black Numbers

    • Adult Sidecar Classes

  • All - White Backgrounda and Black Numbers

    • Youth Solo Classes

  • 50cc Automatics - Yellow Plates with Black Numbers
  • 65cc Cadeets - Whate Plates with Black Numbers
  • 65cc Juniors - Black Plates with White Numbers
  • 86cc Small Wheel - Red Plates with White Numbers
  • 85cc Big Wheel - Green Plates with White Numbers
  • Intermediate - Blue Plates with White Numbers
  • Premier - Black Plates with White Numbers

    • Youth Quad Classes

  • All - White Background and Black Numbers
